# Introducing GPT-6 Sol and Luna

Source URL: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/
Retrieved: 2026-09-23

OpenAI announced GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna on 2026-09-22. The API models are `gpt-6-sol` and `gpt-6-luna`; Sol is priced at US$2/M input and US$10/M output tokens, while Luna is US$0.10/M input and US$0.50/M output. OpenAI describes these as 50% lower prices than GPT-5.6 Sol and Luna, respectively, and says both are available in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API with a gradual rollout.

OpenAI positions Sol for complex professional/coding work and Luna for high-volume, well-defined tasks. Its benchmark comparisons are first-party and depend on the selected effort level and harness. Codex CLI 0.156.1 adds Sol and Luna to its model picker and recommends Luna on a rate-limit switch prompt.

Independent early analysis from Artificial Analysis, as reported by The Decoder, found the main change to be lower cost: Sol rose slightly on the Coding Agent Index while Luna declined slightly, with both showing mixed gains and regressions on other evaluations. The evidence does not establish a blanket quality upgrade over GPT-5.6; it supports a cost-focused routing trial.
