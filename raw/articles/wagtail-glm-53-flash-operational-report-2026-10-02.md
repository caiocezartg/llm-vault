Wagtail CMS published a one-month engineering report on 2 October 2026 after attempting to use GLM-5.3-Flash as its only efficient open model during September. The team consumed roughly 2B tokens in the month; it used GLM-5.3-Flash for about the first half, reporting USD 68 and an estimated 4 kWh for that portion, then used other models for about 1B tokens. The team attributes the deviation to an expensive vibe-coded MCP prototype, continuing R&D/benchmark activity, and provider availability/performance degradation; it switched among GLM-5.3-Flash, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and Qwen 3.8 Flash. Its account is a real operational signal, not a controlled comparison or a general cost claim. The report recommends local measurement of spend, energy and accepted outcomes, explicit R&D budgets, and role-bounded agent workflows.
