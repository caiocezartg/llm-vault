# Introducing Gemini 3.8 Flash and 3.8 Flash Cyber — bounded primary-source record

Google announced Gemini 3.8 Flash on 2026-09-02 as a general-purpose model for long-horizon software engineering, autonomous agents, and multi-step reasoning. The announcement lists introductory API pricing of US$0.75 per million input tokens and US$3.75 per million output tokens through 2026-12-31, then US$1.50 and US$7.50 respectively.

Google documents a 1M-token context window, 64k maximum output tokens, `low`/`medium`/`high` thinking levels, and production availability through Gemini API. The vendor reports gains over 3.7 Flash on its coding and agentic evaluations; these performance claims remain vendor evidence pending controlled, like-for-like evaluation.

GitHub’s 2026-09-03 changelog separately confirms staged Copilot availability in VS Code, Visual Studio, Copilot CLI, cloud agent, Copilot app, JetBrains IDEs, Xcode, and Eclipse. The rollout is gradual.
