Title: GPT-6 Astra: A new generation of intelligence

URL Source: https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-astra/
Published: 2026-09-03

Markdown Content:
OpenAI announced GPT-6 Astra as a frontier model for computer use, browsing, software engineering, cybersecurity, science, and professional work. The company states that availability begins with a limited organizational rollout and expands to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, the OpenAI API, Azure, and AWS Bedrock.

The published standard API price is US$10 per million input tokens and US$50 per million output tokens. OpenAI reports results across its own selected evaluations, including Terminal-Bench Science, Agents' Last Exam, OSWorld 2.0, and coding; these are first-party comparisons and must not be treated as deployment guarantees.

OpenAI also reports stricter safeguards for advanced cyber capability. The claim is material to evaluation and deployment policy, but this raw capture does not infer the effectiveness of those safeguards beyond the source.

Independent evaluation published on the launch date by Artificial Analysis measured GPT-6 Astra at 67 in its Coding Agent Index in the Codex harness (versus 65.1 for GPT-5.6 Sol), with strong token-efficiency gains. The same analysis measured a 61 tie with Sol in its broader Intelligence Index and a higher cost per task at max effort due to the 2.5x list-price increase. Model choice therefore still requires fixed-harness testing on retained tasks.
