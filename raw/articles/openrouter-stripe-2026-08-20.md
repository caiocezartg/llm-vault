# OpenRouter is Joining Stripe

Source: https://openrouter.ai/blog/announcements/openrouter-is-joining-stripe/
Published: 2026-08-19

OpenRouter announced that it is joining Stripe, subject to customary closing conditions expected to conclude in coming weeks. The company says OpenRouter will continue with the same name, product, roadmap and integration surface; it describes itself as a model marketplace/gateway with one interface, provider choice, model-agnostic observability, cost management and routing. It claims to process 10+ trillion tokens daily from 400+ models for over 10 million developers and companies.

The announcement frames the transaction as support for an ecosystem where multiple models and providers remain accessible through one layer. It says routing will remain driven by users' interests and cites Stripe's financial infrastructure, customer network and fraud/abuse expertise as resources to accelerate the product. These are company commitments and claims, not independently verified guarantees about future neutrality, pricing, privacy or availability.
