# LFM2.5-2.6B — primary-source excerpt

Liquid AI announced LFM2.5-2.6B on 2026-08-04 as an open-weight, 2.69B-parameter model post-trained for agentic workloads. The model card documents a 131,072-token context window, four tool-use steps, and native/quantized artifacts for Transformers, vLLM, SGLang, llama.cpp, MLX and ONNX.

The vendor describes a post-training sequence of supervised fine-tuning, domain-specialist teachers, multi-domain on-policy distillation and agentic RL. The latter was conducted in sandboxed harnesses, including Hermes Agent. Vendor-reported performance includes 220 tok/s on an Apple M5 Max and 113 tok/s on Ryzen AI Max+ 395 under 2.5 GB, plus benchmark comparisons with larger models.

Important scope limitation from the model card: Liquid recommends it for tool use, extraction, RAG and long-context workflows, and explicitly does not recommend it for agentic coding or knowledge-heavy tasks. Vendor benchmarks therefore do not establish an outcome for a coding-agent workflow; a constrained local trial is required.

Source: Liquid AI, “LFM2.5-2.6B: Deploy Agents Everywhere,” 2026-08-04. Model card: https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM2.5-2.6B
