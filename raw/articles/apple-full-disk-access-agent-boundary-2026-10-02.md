Apple announced on 2 October 2026 that it will introduce additional macOS controls for Full Disk Access (FDA). FDA largely bypasses normal app privacy controls so backup software can operate; Apple says some apps have used it in ways that can expose files, mail, messages and browsing history without users fully understanding the permission. Apple explicitly links the risk to increasingly capable, autonomous AI agents and says an app that receives FDA will require very explicit user action. The announcement does not specify the final API or UX mechanics. Independent reporting by Ars Technica connects the timing to a dispute about a general-purpose agent’s access to Messages; that attribution is not named by Apple and remains reporting context, not an Apple finding.
