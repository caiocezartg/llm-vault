# OneCLI v2.0.0 release

Source: https://github.com/onecli/onecli/releases/tag/v2.0.0
Published: 2026-08-18

OneCLI published v2.0.0. The official release describes its material change as "workspaces, split services, hosted agents". Version v2.0.1 followed the same day with bug fixes for chat-native approval cards, cross-surface synchronization and channel seams, plus restoration of a GitHub-detectable Apache-2.0 license. The public repository is Apache-2.0 and describes the product as an open-source sandboxed agent harness for teams.

The release notes do not establish that the new hosted-agent or approval architecture is safe or production-ready. The technical boundary still requires verification of credential injection, method/path/request policy granularity, egress restrictions, sandbox isolation, approval binding and failure-closed behavior.
