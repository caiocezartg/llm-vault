# Bounded source capture — Meta: Muse Spark Open

Source published 2026-10-15. Meta announced downloadable Muse Spark Open weights, a natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model positioned for coding and long-horizon agentic work. It reports 816B total parameters, 42B active parameters, and a 1M-token context window.

The announcement says weights are available through Hugging Face, Amazon SageMaker AI, Google Cloud Model Garden, and Microsoft Foundry Models; it lists hosted access and support across multiple inference engines. Meta demonstrates local use on two M5 Ultra Mac Studios, but that is a vendor demonstration rather than an independent task-quality result.

The associated Muse Model License permits many uses but has conditions, including attribution and an additional written agreement for Model-as-a-Service offerings by organizations above the stated revenue threshold. A deployer remains responsible for agent/tool authority, safety and operational evaluation.
