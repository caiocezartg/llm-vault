# OpenAI — Research acceleration: The view inside OpenAI

Source date: 2026-09-06

OpenAI reports an internal snapshot of coding-agent use in its research organization. It says that by mid-August the median researcher was using coding agents daily and the 90th-percentile user consumed more than US$7,000 per day of inference at API prices. It reports 3.1 agent-workdays of runtime per human workday across the organization, counting direct agents and downstream subagents.

The company says increased coding-agent use is correlated with more code and experiments, but explicitly notes that this is not enough to isolate causation because compute capacity also increased. Its task-outcome analysis says agent success rose from January through July across several difficulty buckets, while human steering remained material: over half of successful tasks estimated at four to eight hours involved at least one intervention.

The source frames its measurements as preliminary and company-internal. Its portable implication for engineering is not a general productivity multiplier: use concurrent workers only with defined task boundaries, intervention/escalation paths, and independent acceptance evidence.
